Place your project images here.

Recommended structure:
  assets/images/hero.jpg
  assets/images/about.jpg
  assets/images/projects/<slug>-cover.jpg
  assets/images/projects/<slug>-1.jpg
  assets/images/services/<slug>.jpg

Then reference them in data/site-data.js with relative paths, e.g.
  image: "assets/images/hero.jpg"
